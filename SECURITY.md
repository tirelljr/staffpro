# Security Policy

The Staff Pro BPO team takes security issues seriously. To report a security issue, please go through the information mentioned [here](https://frappe.io/security).

You can help us make Staff Pro BPO and all its users more secure by following the [Reporting guidelines](https://frappe.io/security).

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings. We'll endeavor to respond quickly, and will keep you updated throughout the process.
